Our Story

Built on African Ambition

Where It Began

Founded on a Simple Belief

World-class luxury automobiles don't need to be imported. Kaksju Cars was founded to prove that African engineers, designers, and craftspeople could build vehicles that rival any global luxury brand — while staying rooted in African identity.

Today, our facilities employ thousands of skilled workers across the continent, and every Kaksju vehicle carries that shared pride from the assembly line to the open road.
